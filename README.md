# agapeme ❤️‍🔥

*Sacrificial-self-giving love held as a received ordinance — love that descends to the unworthy at ultimate cost and creates worth in the beloved by the act of loving.*

agapeme is two true roots welded into one word: the Greek **agapē** (ἀγάπη), the love the New Testament loaded most heavily — God descending to the unworthy creature at ultimate cost, unmotivated by merit — and the Sumerian **me** (𒈨), the eldest word for a fundamental that was *received*, not made. So agapeme is agape held as a decree: love whose very capacity is given before it can be offered back. Honest about its own root, it does not pretend to an ancient pedigree — *agapē* the noun was nearly absent from classical Greek, a near-blank vessel the Septuagint and New Testament authors filled late with the content of the cross.

agapeme names the **costliest register** in the love-of-DIVINE convergence: love that gives itself away for the beloved's sake, unmotivated by worthiness, creating value through the act of loving. Where its sibling **ifeqing** is the warmth that widens and **ahavame** is commanded-disposition, agapeme is the love that bleeds — voluntary kenosis, received before practiced (*we love because He first loved us*). Its light is love that no merit triggered and no withdrawal can revoke; its shadow is self-erasure — kenosis with no self left to give, or self-giving kept on a ledger to be repaid. Forged in the cathedral at weighted **7.40/10** (Specialized).
